01 What we do

Five cells.
One obsession.

Everything we run is hands-on. You will not sit through slides about what a buffer overflow is — you will pop one in week two.

/ 01

Web

Injection, auth flaws, SSRF, template injection and deserialisation. OWASP patterns against real, broken stacks.

WebOWASP
/ 02

Reverse

Ghidra, x64dbg and Frida. Stripped binaries, custom VMs and packed samples taken apart until they make sense.

GhidraFrida
/ 03

Pwn

Stack and heap fundamentals, ROP, format strings, and every mitigation you will have to bypass.

PwnROP
/ 04

Cloud

Cloud attack surface, IAM misconfiguration, containers, CI/CD, and the metadata paths everyone forgets.

CloudIAM
/ 05

Pentesting & Bug Bounty

End-to-end engagements: recon, exploitation, and a report a triager will actually accept.

ReconReporting

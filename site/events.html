02 Formats

Four ways to train.

Each format exists for a different reason. Mixing them is what makes a cohort sharp.

01

Workshops

One technique, hands on keyboards. Slides are banned after the first ten minutes.

02

Open labs

Unstructured practice with mentors in the room. Bring whatever you're stuck on.

03

Talks

Members and guests present research, war stories and career paths, plus questions.

04

Competitions

Internal ladders and external CTFs. Teams are mixed by skill level on purpose.

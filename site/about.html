01 Origin

A club built the way 1337 builds software.

StyxSec was founded in 2026 by six students who shared a complaint: the campus had plenty of people learning to code, and almost nobody learning how that code gets broken.

So we built the thing we wanted to attend. No lectures. A lab with live targets. A weekly slot where somebody shows a technique on a real machine and then everybody tries it.

The club is one month old. That is not a weakness we are hiding — it is the reason everything on this site is either a fact or marked as still to come.

02 Principles

How we operate.

Four rules that will outlast the founding board. They are why people stay.

/ 01

Show, don't tell

Every session ends with hands on a keyboard. If a technique can't be demonstrated on a live target, it isn't ready to be taught.

Rule 01
/ 02

Everyone starts somewhere

No gatekeeping. First-years get a structured track and a mentor. The only requirement is showing up.

Rule 02
/ 03

Publish what you find

Findings go in public writeups. Knowledge that stays in a private chat dies with the cohort.

Rule 03
/ 04

Ethics, always

Written scope before every engagement. Responsible disclosure, no exceptions. We break what we're allowed to break.

03 The cells

Pick a river branch.

Onboarding covers the fundamentals for everyone. After that, members join one of five cells — four technical, plus pentesting and bug bounty.

Choose your cell
Web

Injection, authentication flaws, SSRF, template injection and deserialisation. OWASP patterns and bug-bounty methodology against real stacks.

Reverse

Static and dynamic analysis with Ghidra, x64dbg and Frida. Stripped binaries, custom VMs and packed samples.

Pwn

Stack and heap fundamentals, ROP, format strings, mitigations and how to bypass them — escalating from ret2win to heap grooming.

Cloud

Cloud attack surface, IAM misconfiguration, containers, CI/CD pipelines, and the metadata paths people forget.

Pentesting & Bug Bounty

End-to-end engagements: scoping, recon, exploitation, and a report a triager will actually accept. Led by Ztafjout.

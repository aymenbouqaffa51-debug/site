04 Rules

The short version.

The five rules that will not be negotiable when the first edition runs. Full rules go to registered teams before the event.

Attack the challenges, not each other

Any attack against the scoreboard, the infrastructure hosting it, or another team's machine is grounds for immediate disqualification. One exception: the designated King-of-the-Hill box, if announced.

No flag sharing

Flags are per-team. Sharing a flag with another team forfeits both teams' points for that challenge.

Internet is allowed, AI assistants are allowed

This is a real-world simulation. Documentation, search engines and AI assistants are all fair game. Reading another team's written solution mid-event is not.

Submit early, submit often

No penalty for wrong submissions, but rate limiting applies after 20 attempts on a single challenge. Dynamic scoring means points decay as more teams solve a challenge.

Be the kind of person we want in the lab

Beginners are welcome and expected. If you finish early and see someone stuck, help them — the community award is worth more than third place.

Not competing? Come watch.

Spectators will be welcome all day. A beginner track runs in parallel with a dedicated mentor for first-time CTF players.

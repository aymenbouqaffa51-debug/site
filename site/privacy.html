Privacy

StyxSec keeps no member database. Signing in with 42 gives your browser a short-lived session, and nothing more is stored on our side.

What we receive from 42

When you press “Continue with 42” and approve, 42 sends our login service your public profile: intra id, login, first and last name, email, campus, cursus level, wallet, evaluation points, pool month and year, and the link to your photo. We never see your 42 password.

Where it lives

In one signed, HttpOnly cookie in your browser, set by api.styxsec.tech and valid for 7 days. There is no database behind it, so we do not hold a copy. The campus is checked once at sign-in to confirm you belong to 1337 Rabat.

Your photo

The dashboard shows your 42 photo. It is fetched through our login service from 42’s servers and is not saved.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and the login service runs on Cloudflare, so both see ordinary request logs such as IP address and user agent.

Leave or delete

Use Sign out on your dashboard to remove the session at once. Because nothing is stored on our side, that is a complete removal. For anything else, write to contact@styxsec.tech.

Found a security problem?

Please tell us. Our contact and policy are in security.txt.
